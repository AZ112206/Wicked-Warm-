About the project

A full-stack forecast for your energy bill

Wicked Warm helps homeowners see a reliable estimate of next month's energy bill before it arrives — and what to do when it's trending up. It's built as a full-stack application: a browser front end, a backend that runs the prediction, and an AI layer that turns the forecast into a plain-language action plan.

The problem

Bills arrive too late to act on

By the time the bill shows up, the month is already spent. Families are left reacting instead of planning. Wicked Warm flips that: it forecasts the bill in advance and updates daily, so there's time to make small changes that add up.

How the stack works

Front end · backend · AI

🖥️

Front end

The pages you're using now — home, about, and the price tracker — built with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

⚙️

Backend

Takes your inputs and usage history, pulls weather and climate signals, and runs the model that forecasts your bill.

🤖

AI action plan

Turns the forecast into specific, tailored recommendations when your bill is predicted to rise.

Inside a prediction

What the model weighs

📊

Your history

Up to five years of your usage and billing patterns.

🌡️

Weather

Past and forecasted temperatures driving heating and cooling.

🌊

Climate cycles

Seasonal patterns like El Niño and La Niña.

🏠

Your home

Location, household size, and home type.
